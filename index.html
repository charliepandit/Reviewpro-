FAQ
Common questions.
How fast can I go live?
+
Under 60 seconds. Pick a plan, fill basic info, verify your phone with OTP, and your QR code + dashboard activate instantly. No manual approval.
Is this against Google's rules?
+
Review Pro never writes a review about an experience the customer did not have, and never posts anything on their behalf. The customer taps what was true about their visit, the AI phrases it, and they post it themselves from their own phone — free to edit or discard it. What it must not be used for is reviews from people who were never your customers, or reviews traded for a discount. That is what Google acts on.
Do I need to give my Google Place ID?
+
Just paste your Google Maps shop link — Review Pro will auto-extract the Place ID for you. If it can't, you can enter it manually with our guide.
What if I want to cancel?
+
No problem. No contracts. Just message us on WhatsApp. No penalty, no hidden charges.
Can I change the review language later?
+
Yes. English, Hindi, Hinglish — switchable anytime from your dashboard settings.
What's the refund policy?
+
The 2-day trial is there so you can test it properly before paying — no card needed. If something goes wrong after you have paid, message us on WhatsApp and we will sort it out.

📍 How to get your Google Place ID
💡 The easiest way: just paste your Google Maps shop link in the registration form. Review Pro auto-extracts the Place ID. The steps below are for manual entry.
1
Open Google Maps and search for your shop by name.
2
Tap your shop → tap Share → tap Copy link.
3
Paste the link in the Google Maps Link field. We auto-extract the Place ID.

Example link:
https://www.google.com/maps/place/.../data=!1sChIJu1JZJAPhdTkRgfOUwL9sUhU!...
4
Manual lookup: Go to Google's Place ID Finder, search your business, copy the ID (starts with ChIJ) and paste in the Place ID field.
