FAQ
Common questions.
Is this against Google's rules?
+
No. Customers share their genuine experience — the AI just helps them find the right words. They post from their own phone. It's 100% Google policy compliant.
How long does setup take?
+
Within 24 hours of payment, your QR code is ready. The UP75 Team personally handles setup and confirms via WhatsApp.
Does the customer need to download an app?
+
No. Scanning the QR opens directly in the phone browser. No app, no registration.
What if Google deletes reviews?
+
Every review is different — words, structure, language mix. Google doesn't find a pattern. For safety, keep it to 2-3 reviews per day.
What if I want to cancel?
+
No problem. No contracts. Just message us on WhatsApp. No penalty, no hidden charges.
Can I change the review language later?
+
Yes. English, Hindi, Hinglish — switchable anytime from your dashboard settings. Your AI will adapt immediately.
